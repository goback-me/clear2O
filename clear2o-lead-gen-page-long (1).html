The problem with point-of-use filters

One jug filter can't cover a whole property

Fridge filters and under-sink units only treat the water at that one point. Everywhere else - showers, laundry, outdoor taps - is still running straight off the mains.

Heavy metals

Lead, mercury and iron entering from ageing mains infrastructure are reduced right at the point of entry.

Chlorine & VOCs

Treatment chemicals and volatile organics are pulled out before water ever reaches a tap.

Mineral load

Scale-forming minerals that clog pipes and coat appliances are controlled across the whole system.

Microplastics

Fine particulate filtration captures what most single-point filters simply pass straight through.
